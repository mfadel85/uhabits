## Troubleshooting:

### **Dashboard Not Updating?**
1. Force close and reopen the app
2. Check that you have active (non-archived) habits
3. Verify the habit you're checking is actually being marked complete
4. Try completing a different habit to test

### **Score Still Shows "---"?**
- This means you have no active habits
- Create at least one habit first
- Dashboard will start showing scores once habits exist

### **Performance Seems Wrong?**
- Score is calculated across ALL active habits
- Numerical habits get partial credit based on target achievement
- Bonus points are awarded for streaks and consistency
- Weekend completions get bonus points
